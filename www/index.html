Privacy Policy

What Nova stores. When you sign up we store your name, your email and the last time you used Nova.

Why. To create your account and let you log in. We do not sell your data.

Who can see it. You can see your own details. Only the Nova owner (the admin) can see the list of users.

On your phone. Notes, settings and your AI keys stay on your device. If you turn on screen reading, the text on your screen is sent to the AI provider you chose so it can explain it. Nova does not keep it.

Your choices. You can turn off screen reading, message reading, the floating button and the wake word in Settings at any time. To delete your account and data, contact the Nova owner.

By creating an account you agree to this policy.
